SUPPLEMENTARY FILE S2 - SEARCH STRATEGY (PRISMA-S)
Deep Learning for Semen Analysis in Male Infertility: a systematic literature map

All searches were executed programmatically on 29 July 2026 (single run date for every
source). Coverage window 2020-01-01 to 2026-07-29. Language limited to English at the
screening stage, not by API parameter. No citation chaining (backward/forward) was
performed; this is stated as a limitation in the manuscript.

Scripts that reproduce every step, in execution order:
  search_results/run_searches.py       sources 1-5
  search_results/run_s2.py             Semantic Scholar re-run
  search_results/run_surrogates.py     IEEE-published records
  search_results/enrich_abstracts.py   abstract back-fill
  search_results/screen.py             deduplication + title/abstract screening
  search_results/eligibility.py        eligibility assessment + thematic coding
  search_results/verify_dois.py        Crossref metadata + retraction check
  search_results/finalize.py           corpus assembly
  build_supplements.py                 this file and S1/S3/S4
  figures/make_manuscript_figures.py   Figure 2
  prisma_vml.py                        Figure 1

1. EUROPE PMC
Endpoint : https://www.ebi.ac.uk/europepmc/webservices/rest/search
Params   : format=json, pageSize=100, resultType=core, cursorMark pagination (max 4 pages/query)
Records  : 2,732
Queries (7), each appended with AND (FIRST_PDATE:[2020-01-01 TO 2026-12-31]):
  Q1 (("deep learning" OR "convolutional neural network" OR "neural network" OR
       "artificial intelligence" OR "machine learning") AND ("semen analysis" OR
       spermatozoa OR sperm))
  Q2 (("deep learning" OR CNN OR YOLO OR transformer) AND (sperm AND (morphology OR
       motility OR concentration OR detection OR tracking OR segmentation)))
  Q3 (("artificial intelligence" OR "deep learning") AND ("male infertility" OR
       azoospermia OR oligozoospermia OR asthenozoospermia OR teratozoospermia))
  Q4 (("deep learning" OR "artificial intelligence") AND ("sperm selection" OR
       "sperm retrieval" OR "micro-TESE" OR ICSI))
  Q5 (("computer-assisted sperm analysis" OR CASA) AND ("deep learning" OR
       "machine learning" OR "artificial intelligence"))
  Q6 (("smartphone" OR "point-of-care" OR "home-based") AND ("semen analysis" OR sperm)
       AND ("deep learning" OR "artificial intelligence" OR "machine learning"))
  Q7 (("DNA fragmentation" OR "sperm DNA") AND ("deep learning" OR
       "convolutional neural network"))

3. PUBMED
Endpoint : NCBI E-utilities esearch.fcgi then esummary.fcgi (db=pubmed, retmax=2000)
Records  : 596
Query (verbatim):
  (("deep learning"[tiab] OR "artificial intelligence"[tiab] OR "machine learning"[tiab]
    OR "neural network*"[tiab] OR "convolutional"[tiab])
   AND ("semen"[tiab] OR "sperm*"[tiab] OR "spermatozoa"[MeSH Terms]
        OR "Semen Analysis"[MeSH Terms]))
  AND ("2020/01/01"[PDAT] : "2026/12/31"[PDAT])
Note: esummary returns no abstract field. Abstracts for on-topic records were
back-filled from Europe PMC before screening (289 of 324 recovered); this is reported
in the manuscript because it changed the included set.

DEDUPLICATION
Primary key DOI (lower-cased, https://doi.org/ prefix stripped). Records without a DOI
were keyed on a normalised title (lower-cased, markup and non-alphanumerics removed,
whitespace collapsed). A second pass collapsed DOI-less records whose normalised title
matched a DOI-bearing record. Where duplicates carried different metadata richness, the
record with an abstract, then the higher citation count, was retained; source
provenance was unioned. 2,018 duplicates removed from 5,348 records.
